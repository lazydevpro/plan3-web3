PLAN³ / product strategy / 28 September 2026

A better builder starts with a sharper job.

The opportunity is not another screen full of crypto data. It is a research workspace that preserves how the evidence connects to a decision—and what would change it.

01 / Competitive map

Learn from the leaders. Be honest about the gaps.

Official documentation reviewed, not a paid-account feature benchmark. A feature absent from a page is not proven absent from a product. Source breadth, professional charting and hosted collaboration remain weaknesses for Plan3.

Two signals worth remembering

Context confusion is a usability risk.

A 2026 Reddit discussion describes confusion around linked chart settings. One anecdote cannot establish broad demand or a current bug. It does support making binding state visible and easy to reverse.

Standalone terminals need a durable job.

Kraken announced Cryptowatch’s sunset in 2023 and feature integration into Kraken Pro. The documented fact is consolidation, not a proven design failure. Our inference: aggregation alone is a vulnerable position.

02 / Research translated into code

From fixed cards to editable instruments.

IMPLEMENTED

Compose the data and the view

Seven custom building blocks: metric, history chart, watchlist, ranking, condition, thesis/note and reference link. Select known fields or inspectable derived ratios. Preview the actual widget before adding it.

IMPLEMENTED

Link context without surprises

Custom instruments can follow the board asset or stay pinned. Repeat instances have independent settings. Existing specialist CMC presets keep their explicit scope.

IMPLEMENTED

Keep the reasoning on the canvas

Connect evidence with supports, contradicts, watches and derived-from assertions. Conditions show the formula, threshold, current result and unknown state when data is unavailable.

IMPLEMENTED

Make iteration safe

Multiple local boards, templates, duplication, undo/redo, precise geometry, free dragging, resizing, pan and zoom. Existing board content is migrated instead of deleted.

IMPLEMENTED

A shared human–agent manifest

Compatible browser agents can read the same structured board and stage a proposal. A human must accept it. The included starter is clearly labelled rule-based, not an LLM.

IMPLEMENTED

Share the workflow

Export/import validated JSON, send a read-only configuration snapshot, and remix it into a local board. The site remains private; market values refresh on opening.

03 / The next product bets

The hard work still ahead.

  1. Composable connectors: schemas, entity/timeframe parameters, units, provenance, freshness and credit budgets. Broaden configurable CMC coverage before adding another source.
  2. A model-backed research agent: cited retrieval, visible per-widget change review, cost estimates, atomic apply and rollback. Test untrusted-source injection.
  3. Reusable research playbooks: parameters and decision journals, with timestamp-correct outcome tracking. No predictive-performance claims without evidence.
  4. Hosted monitoring and collaboration: authenticated persistence, scheduled checks, opt-in delivery, revisions and concurrent edits.
  5. A curated template network: attribution, requirements and remix tracking. Potential future defensibility, not an existing moat.

Earn “best” with observed use.

Test with 5–8 target researchers against their own current workflow: reproduce a board, compare BTC/SOL, pin a widget, express invalidation, share/remix, and recover a mistake.

Targets—not achieved claims: a useful custom board in under five minutes; at least 80% unassisted task completion; no binding confusion or lost edits; three workflows reused weekly. Measure second-session return, accepted versus rolled-back agent proposals, source errors and credits per active board.

Build a living market thesis.Compose your data, connect your evidence, and let an agent propose the next change—on a board you control.
